# Gate decision: Empirical feasibility revision

## Purpose and precedence

This append-only decision records how the evidence in 04-semantics-spike.md and corrected 05-transport-spike.md changes the planning contract. It applies the supersession mapping already established by 14-observation-criteria.md: AC-019 refines AC-003 and AC-006; AC-021 refines AC-005; AC-022 adds the application-scoped observe surface without replacing page read/act; AC-026 supersedes only the page-disposal delivery clause of AC-016; and 13-observation-plan.md refines the matching observation and lifecycle choices in 01-plan.md. Every clause not expressly superseded below remains required, including privacy, security, authorization, fail-closed behavior, generator scope, manual fallback, measurement, and native proof.

The two spike-level FAIL verdicts do not have one uniform consequence. Evidence that invalidates a proposed behavior revises the behavior before implementation. Evidence unavailable only because a required external credential was absent blocks a support or completion claim, not development against independently demonstrated conformance. Evidence that depends on components which do not yet exist moves to implementation verification without changing its protective limit.

## Decision summary

Implementation may proceed only after this revised plan and criteria receive a fresh blind validation and the criteria are frozen. The implementable MVP is limited to one Flutter view and Chrome 152 behavior demonstrated by JavaScript and Wasm page-conformance runs. Native publication remains unsupported and undelivered until the authenticated Chrome-only natural-language trace passes. No current artifact claims that implementation, support, or delivery exists.

## Support and delivery boundary

Page conformance establishes a development target, not a native agent support claim. Before final support, release, or completion, an authenticated Chrome-only natural-language agent must discover the published tools, observe the eligible page, read it, select and act on a permitted control, navigate, receive the app-owned dispatch receipt, observe the new eligible page, and read that page. The trace must use the required inspector profile and actual model-selected tool calls. Any absent credential, disabled prompt, simulated choice, direct in-page invocation, WebDriver command, or manually assembled transcript leaves the claim blocked.

The browser capability surface must report the distinction between cancel-before-dispatch and unavailable invocation-time cancellation after dispatch. Library-owned waiter, receipt, and operation records remain bounded. Cancellation, timeout, page disposal, or app detach may stop library delivery and future dispatch, but cannot be described as terminating an already running application callback or external side effect. Retryable status never authorizes automatic replay.

## Criteria and implementation gate

The revised acceptance contract is 02-criteria.md plus 14-observation-criteria.md plus 17-gate-revision-criteria.md, interpreted using the clause-level supersessions in the newest artifact. It remains unfrozen until a new blind plan validation round accepts 16-gate-revision-plan.md and 17-gate-revision-criteria.md. Implementation remains blocked until that validation and freeze, even though the empirical decisions above remove the earlier need to prove unsupported capabilities before development.

Native support, delivery, publication documentation, and overall work-item completion remain blocked until the authenticated trace and all applicable implementation verification checks pass. Optional generator work remains part of the planned full scope and cannot be silently removed by declaring the runtime MVP complete.
