## Explicitly not required

**Chosen not to demonstrate — supported features, out of scope by D-0014-04.** A custom
`WebMcpViewProvider`; customized `WebMcpPageLimits`; nested or parallel `WebMcpNavigatorAdapter`
usage through the parent-navigator, persistent-parallel-branch or selected-branch parameters; the
page widget's explicit activity listenable; cursor-paginated page reads; and dedicated
demonstrations of the evidence-kind enumeration, the page protocol version constant beyond a single
rendered row, and the maximum-safe-integer constant. These are real, working features. Their
absence is a scope decision and no criterion here may describe them as prohibited.

**Contract-level deliberate omissions — different bucket.** Everything on the deliberate-omissions
list in `wiki/product/webmcp-contract.md` remains omitted. One of them, the positive observation
wait, is surfaced as an explicit refusal by AC-022 rather than being hidden; that is a demonstration
of the refusal, not of the feature.

**Also not required.** Forcing `document.modelContext` to appear in Chrome, or establishing any
particular native support level (D-0014-03); proving real AI-agent tool use, which the product
contract disclaims for manual console invocation; adding a generator stage for `example/` to
`tools/check.sh`, which stays a recorded follow-up; any change under `lib/`; any package version
change; a re-verification of the Flutter or Dart toolchain version, which is already resolved in the
decision log and is not what AC-036 or AC-037 check.
