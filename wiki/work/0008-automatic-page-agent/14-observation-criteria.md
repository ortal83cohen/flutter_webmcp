## Supersession mapping

AC-019 refines AC-003 and AC-006: a minimal app installs one shared session and a separate adapter for each relevant Navigator, plus an opted-in boundary per page; observer registration alone cannot expose content. AC-021 refines AC-005 by distinguishing content revisions from immediate eligibility invalidation. AC-022 adds one app-scoped observe tool without changing the page read/act schemas. AC-026 supersedes only the page-disposal delivery portion of AC-016 and the matching baseline plan sentence: new dispatch ends immediately, but an already admitted app-owned operation may deliver its bounded receipt after its originating page disappears. All other original privacy, action, source-lifetime, cancellation, browser, compatibility, measurement, and proof requirements remain in force. 13-observation-plan.md supersedes corresponding observation/lifecycle choices in 01-plan.md; 15-observation-tasks.md extends 03-tasks.md rather than cancelling its tasks.

## Criteria

| ID | Criterion | How it is checked | Negative case |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-019 | When automatic observation is enabled, one app session shall share one metadata broker across separate per-Navigator adapters, semantic boundaries, and the agent command dispatcher; existing consumer observers, Actions dispatchers, transports, and explicit tools shall retain their behavior. | Minimal consumer fixture installs the session, one root adapter and one page wrapper without per-control registration; composition tests record consumer callbacks and manual behavior. | Reusing one observer across Navigators, replacing a consumer observer/dispatcher, or enabling exposure without a proven boundary fails validation. |
| AC-020 | When a root, nested, shell, or branch route changes, an active branch changes without a route event, or an overlay changes modality, affected scopes shall lose eligibility before the next frame; only a proven selected branch and uncovered settled route with a live owned semantics boundary may resume read/act. | Navigation matrix covers root/nested observers, explicit branch selection, root modal over a branch, wrapped/unwrapped modals, back/history/redirect, transition completion and interruption. | A retained handle during a gesture, transition, unknown branch, missing adapter, or ambiguous blocking overlay cannot read old content or dispatch; one completed frame alone cannot reactivate a transitioning page. |
| AC-021 | When semantics or an opted-in signal notifies, the system shall mark the affected projection dirty, coalesce capture work, reproject after an eligible frame within a deadline, and advance content revision only when the permitted immutable projection changes; eligibility/policy/source changes shall independently invalidate stale actions immediately. | Rebuild, same-value notification, semantic change, owner replacement, source replacement, privacy change, and semantics enable/disable fixtures inspect revisions, invalidation, and cleanup. | Unchanged notifications do not invent content changes or causal success; missing frames return snapshotUnavailable within 2 seconds; obsolete callbacks cannot publish after replacement/disposal. |
| AC-022 | When an agent calls the proposed version-one app observe tool, it shall receive bounded metadata and safe references only to currently eligible opted-in scopes from the same broker; it shall survive page navigation while existing page read/act endpoints remain the content/action interface. | A real native browser-agent trace calls observe, reads/acts on a page, navigates, observes the next page, then reads it; immediate waitMs zero works on every supported transport. | No content, inactive-page reference, custom DOM state event, or WebMCP toolchange claim substitutes for observe; unsupported positive waits reject safely with immediate polling advertised. |
| AC-023 | When observe receives a cursor, it shall distinguish the app mount/event sequence from each page mount/revision and page pagination cursor; malformed, foreign-mount, or evicted cursors shall return gap and refreshRequired with the current app cursor and active references for reconciliation. | App remount, page remount with the same identifier, ring overflow, and fresh read after a gap exercise recovery. | A cursor from pagination, another app mount, or expired history cannot silently imply no change, revive stale page handles, or disclose historical inactive scope identifiers. |
| AC-024 | When own semantic actions or explicit domain calls run, central tracking shall report commandDispatched, visibleEffectObserved, domainFutureCompleted, and backendConfirmed as separate evidence kinds; only an explicit operation-linked signal may establish causality or backend confirmation. | Delayed, failed, navigation-producing, unchanged-projection, and explicit confirmation fixtures distinguish dispatch, returned Future, temporal change, and correlated confirmation. | A completed frame, quiet interval, unrelated human interaction, observer callback, or successful Future alone never becomes backendConfirmed; uninstrumented callbacks are not reported as captured commands. |
| AC-025 | When optional Listenable/Stream, Flutter Actions, or versioned router/state/proxy adapters attach, they shall declare their coverage, select explicit sources, preserve existing behavior, emit filtered invalidation or named evidence, and remove replaced/disposed subscriptions. | Adapter-specific fixtures include nearer Actions dispatchers, direct callbacks, multiple provider scopes, source identity replacement, and cancellation. Versioned go_router/Bloc/Riverpod support is checked against pinned dependencies only if shipped. | Unselected providers, raw state, direct original service references, and arbitrary callbacks do not become observed automatically; the MVP works without any optional adapter or pointer listener. |
| AC-026 | When an admitted action navigates away or disposes its originating page, the app session shall retain only the bounded operation identity/status needed to return a receipt; disposal rejects all new or queued-not-yet-dispatched page work, and late work cannot revive tools, sources, content, or callbacks. | A synchronous navigation action and an asynchronous explicit domain call both dispose their page; trace permitted receipt delivery, cancellation, timeout, remount, and app detach. | No handler starts after page disposal; a late completion cannot dispatch again or expose old page content. App detach cancels delivery and clears operation tracking. |

## Non-functional criteria

| ID | Criterion | How it is checked | Negative case |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-027 | When observe/events/operations reach resource limits, the system shall enforce the proposed hard design ceilings: 128 live scopes, 32 Navigator adapters, 256 events and 64 KiB total ring storage, 32 events and 16 KiB per observe response including metadata, 8 pending waits per app and 2 per referenced scope, waitMs at most 1,000, one queued capture per scope, 32 operations retained for at most 30 seconds, and 2 seconds per capture deadline. Configuration may lower these ceilings only. | Boundary and one-over-limit fixtures measure all caps, serialization metadata, coalescing, cancellation removal, timeout unchanged replies, ring eviction and operation expiry. These are protective design values, not latency benchmarks. | Oversized registration fails closed without evicting another live owner; waiter/operation saturation returns busy before dispatch; no-session caller tokens cannot reserve capacity; a no-frame or never-completing Future cannot retain tracking indefinitely. |
| AC-028 | When observation emits wire metadata or diagnostics, an allowlist shall exclude labels, values, route names/URLs/arguments, action arguments/results, state objects, errors, and stacks; retained records use opaque identities, revisions, fixed event kinds, safe reason codes/counts and explicit correlation only. Evidence from supported release/native fixtures and full checks shall gate any shipped claim. | Synthetic secrets across every forbidden field inspect ring, observe response, cancellation/timeout/gap paths and logs; archive exact supported SDK/browser versions, commands, outputs and measured overhead with original proof gates. | Unsafe developer identifiers cannot enter observation metadata; inactive historical scopes are redacted; absent release isolation, native navigation trace, or required check output prevents a support/completion claim. |

## Explicitly not required

Runtime implementation in this planning request; universal route-builder injection or callback/method interception; a mandatory pointer or semantics-action listener; automatic attribution of human UI activity; mandatory state-framework dependencies; browser push/subscription semantics; real-agent long-wait support without proof; application session authentication inferred from WebMCP; backend confirmation without an explicit trusted application signal. Original exclusions remain.
